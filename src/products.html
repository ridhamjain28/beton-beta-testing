Engineering Standards

Technical Quality & Architectural Performance

At Beton Lighting, our products are engineered to strict photometric standards. Every fixture undergoes rigorous spectroradiometer testing to ensure color consistency, color fidelity, glare cut-off, and thermal longevity.

01 / Spectrum

Color Temperature (CCT)

2400K - 6500K

Correlated Color Temperature (CCT) measures light warmth in Kelvin (K). Tuned spectrum options span ultra-warm ambient hospitality tones to crisp neutral daylight for focus work environments. Preset spectral binning ensures precise color temperature consistency across all architectural fixtures in a project.

2400K Warm 3000K Amber 4000K Neutral 6500K Daylight
02 / Fidelity

Color Rendering Index (CRI Ra 90+)

Ra > 90

CRI measures how accurately a light source reveals natural colors compared to sunlight (Ra 100). High CRI Ra 90+ LED engines ensure vibrant architectural materials, textiles, and artwork without grey tinting. Enhanced R9 red spectrum values preserve natural wood tones, human skin tones, and rich interior finishes.

03 / Comfort & Metrics

TM-30 & Low Glare Optics (UGR < 19)

UGR < 19

Unified Glare Rating (UGR) quantifies visual glare discomfort. Deep-recessed dark light reflectors keep UGR below 19 to eliminate eye fatigue, backed by IES TM-30 Rf 92+ color fidelity spectrum analysis. Optically engineered baffles and cut-off angles shield direct light sources for supreme visual ergonomics in high-end spaces.

04 / Consistency

MacAdam Ellipse (SDCM < 3)

3-Step Binning

Standard Deviation Color Matching (SDCM) measures color consistency across LED chips. 3-step MacAdam binning guarantees zero visible color hue variance between fixtures installed along continuous walls, coves, and corridors.

SDCM Binning Standards

1-Step SDCM Imperceptible Difference
3-Step SDCM Beton Architectural Standard
5+ Step SDCM Noticeable Variance

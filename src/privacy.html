Privacy Policy

How we handle, secure, and protect your information at Beton Lighting.

1. Overview

At Beton Lighting, we respect your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you browse our catalogue, contact us, or submit an enquiry list.

2. Information Collection

We collect information you directly provide when using our website, specifically via our contact forms and inquiry forms powered by Web3Forms. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company name, city, and files (such as architectural layout drawings or specification documents) you choose to upload.

3. Cookies & Browsing Session

We use temporary cookies and local storage to save your navigation preferences, including selected filter modes and the list of products you add to your enquiry. These cookies persist only during your browsing session and do not track your activity on third-party websites.

4. Data Usage

Your information is used solely to respond to your product inquiries, project consultations, dealer requests, or technical support requests. We do not sell, distribute, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect the integrity of your personal information. However, please be aware that no transmission over the internet or email can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Uploaded files and form inputs are processed securely.

6. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

Any disputes relating to your privacy or data usage on this website are subject to the laws of India, under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Surat, Gujarat.

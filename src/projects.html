Supplied lighting projects, documented with design intent.

This page is designed to showcase projects where Beton has supplied lighting, with each case study explaining what the client needed, how the lighting direction was resolved, and what fixture families supported the final specification.

How to read this page

Each study should focus on the project brief, site condition, lighting approach, product families supplied, and enquiry support. Until verified Beton project data and photography are added, sample imagery is used without client names or completion claims.

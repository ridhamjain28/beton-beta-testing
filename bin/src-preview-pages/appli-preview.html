02 / Efficiency

Commercial
Clarity

Commercial lighting needs efficiency, consistent output, and easy maintenance without making the space feel flat. Beton focuses on low-glare linear systems, controlled beam distribution, and clean integration for offices, showrooms, and shared workspaces.

Technical Specs

  • Low-glare fixture selection
  • DALI or phase-cut control options
  • Neutral CCT for work clarity

Design Focus

Uniformity, visual comfort, maintenance access, and reduced glare across long-use zones.

03 / Productivity

Office

Office lighting needs visual comfort, uniformity, and efficient task illumination for long working hours.

Recommended: linear profiles, panel systems, low-glare downlights.

Consider: glare control, uniformity, desk layouts, energy efficiency.

04 / Visual Focus

Retail

Retail lighting should reveal colour, texture, and product detail while guiding attention through the space.

Recommended: track lights, recessed spotlights, wall washers.

Consider: colour rendering, beam angle, display contrast, maintenance access.
